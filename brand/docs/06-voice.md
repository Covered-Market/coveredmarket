# Voice and tone

Covered writes like a careful broker's letter: plain, exact, a little formal, never selling. It assumes the reader is an adult with money who would like to know what will happen to it.

## Voice (always)

- **Plain.** Short sentences, common words. "Your coins stay on Bitcoin." Not "assets remain natively custodied on the base layer".
- **Exact.** Every claim carries its figure, date or address. "Expires 31 Dec 2026, 08:00 UTC", not "expires at the end of the month".
- **Honest about trust.** Say what the contract can and cannot do, and who signs. "Signatures come from NEAR's MPC network" is a sentence we write in the product, not hide in a footnote.
- **Second person.** You, your coins, your premium. "We" is the team, used rarely, mostly to say what we cannot do: "We never hold your coins."
- **Calm.** No urgency, no scarcity, no countdown copy ("only 3 left"). The Countdown component shows time; the words never push.

## Tone (shifts with the moment)

| Moment | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hero and headlines | Quiet confidence, Fraunces | *Income on Bitcoin you still hold.* |
| Explaining the product | Patient, concrete, one story | "Astrid sells Marcus the right to buy her 2 BTC at $150,000 on 31 December. He pays her $6,000 now. If Bitcoin ends below $150,000, she keeps the coins and the $6,000. If above, she sells at $150,000 and still keeps the $6,000." |
| Inside the app | Terse, labelled, every number present | "Premium $6,000 · paid by marcus.near · received on Base" |
| A refusal | Specific, what to do next | "Deposit refused: 1.9998 BTC received, offer size is 2.0000 BTC. Top up 0.0002 BTC or reduce the size." |
| Social | The same voice, shorter; one fact per post | "Astrid's 2 BTC never left Bitcoin. Her $6,000 premium arrived on Base from Solana. The call expires 31 Dec. Verify it on NEAR." |
| Investor or judge | Mechanism first, then the trade-off we accept | "The coins sit at an address a NEAR contract controls through Chain Signatures. The trust that remains is the MPC network and the price source. That is less than an exchange." |

## Words

Use: coins, hold, premium, strike, expiry, keep, sold at, returned, contract, signs, refused, verify, address, custodian.
Avoid: assets, yield farming, APY (say "annualised"), trustless, seamless, unlock, powered by, secure (as an adjective), guaranteed, risk-free, moon, degen, gm.

## Punctuation and form

- Sentence case for everything, including buttons, headings and slide titles.
- Oxford comma, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no ellipses for effect (the ellipsis is reserved for truncated hashes).
- Buttons are verb first: "Sell this call", "Deposit 2.0000 BTC", "Verify on NEAR".
- Dates `31 Dec 2026`, times `08:00 UTC`, money `$6,000.00` in figures, `2.0000 BTC`.
- The product is "Covered", capital C, never "COVERED" in text and never "Covered Protocol".
